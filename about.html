About Me

Background

I am a Computer Engineering major with a minor in Mathematics at Claflin University, graduating in May 2028. I am a member of the Alice Carson Tisdale Honors College, I also serve as a peer mentor for underclassmen. My experience sits where software, data, and people meet. I've built a health dataset from smartwatch sensor data and designed dashboards to make it easy to read. I've tested evasive command lines to see how well detection systems catch them during cybersecurity research at the University of Virginia. I also interned at BlueCross BlueShield of South Carolina, working with mainframe systems and DB2 databases. On campus, I have served as a teaching assistant, tutor, and Computer Science & Robotics Student Ambassador, and I currently serve as the Academic Excellence Chair for Clafin's chapter of the National Society of Black Engineers. After graduation, I plan to pursue graduate studies in engineering.
