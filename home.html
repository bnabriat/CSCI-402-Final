Briana Nabriat

Biography

Briana Nabriat is a junior majoring in Computer Engineering with a minor in Mathematics from Aiken, SC. She is actively involved on campus as the Academic Excellence Chair of the National Society of Black Engineers, a Computer Science Outreach & Robotics Lead, and a research assistant on multiple technology-focused projects. Briana has earned numerous honors, including Presidential Scholar, BlueScholar, Watson Brown Foundation Scholar, and multiple Dean's and President's List recognitions. Her experiences include cybersecurity research, artificial intelligence research, robotics programming, and data management through internships and research programs. After graduation, Briana plans to pursue graduate studies in engineering.
